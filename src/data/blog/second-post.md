Security work gets expensive when it starts with tools instead of the operating baseline. Most small and mid-sized teams need a simpler order of operations.

## Identity first

Microsoft 365 and Entra ID are often the front door. Before a team buys another platform, it should know whether multifactor authentication is enforced, admin roles are limited, conditional access is sensible, and departed users are fully removed.

## Email and endpoint controls

Email remains one of the easiest ways into a business. Filtering, anti-phishing policy, DMARC alignment, endpoint protection, and device encryption are not exotic. They are the baseline buyers expect a credible provider to discuss clearly.

## Backups and response habits

Backups are only useful if the restore process is known. Response plans are only useful if someone knows who to call, what to isolate, and how leadership gets updated.

The right security posture is not loud. It is visible, testable, and boring in the best possible way.
