Microsoft 365 cleanup looks technical from a distance. Up close, it is a business operations project.

## The mess usually has a reason

Teams channels, SharePoint sites, shared mailboxes, guest users, and file permissions rarely become messy because people are careless. They become messy because the business grew faster than the governance model.

The fix starts with decisions: who owns each workspace, what should be archived, where sensitive files belong, how external sharing works, and how new projects get created.

## Backup and retention are different

Retention policy is not the same as backup. Teams need clarity about what can be recovered, how quickly, and under what conditions. That matters for accidental deletion, legal holds, ransomware, and staff turnover.

## Make the clean state repeatable

The real win is not one cleanup sprint. The win is a repeatable operating model: naming conventions, ownership, lifecycle rules, access review, and a simple way to request new workspaces.

When Microsoft 365 is governed well, people spend less time hunting, duplicating, and second-guessing where work belongs.
