## Challenge

The clinic needed reliable systems across multiple locations while meeting healthcare security expectations. The old environment had enough undocumented history that every support request took longer than it should have.

## Solution

A phased rollout started with access transfer, endpoint visibility, backup verification, and Microsoft 365 review. Larger modernization work waited until the support baseline was no longer guesswork.

## Results

Operations stabilized, vendor handoffs became cleaner, and leadership gained a practical view of what was fixed, what remained exposed, and what belonged in the next budget cycle.
