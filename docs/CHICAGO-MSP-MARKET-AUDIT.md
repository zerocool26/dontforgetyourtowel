# Chicago MSP Market Audit

Last updated: 2026-05-02

## Purpose

This site was rewritten to sound like a credible Chicago-area managed service provider instead of a filler-heavy concept site. The goal was not to mirror one competitor, but to reflect the service architecture, buyer language, and proof patterns that appear repeatedly across leading Chicago MSP firms.

## Repeated Service Patterns Seen Across the Market

These offers appeared consistently across Chicago MSP leaders and ranking pages:

1. Managed IT support and help desk
2. Co-managed IT for internal teams
3. Endpoint, server, and network management
4. Cybersecurity monitoring, EDR/MDR, and identity controls
5. Microsoft 365 administration and cloud support
6. Backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity
7. Strategic consulting, budgeting, and vCIO guidance
8. Compliance support and risk reduction

Second-tier but still believable adjacencies:

1. Workflow automation tied to operations
2. Reporting and executive visibility
3. Client portal, website, or digital experience refresh work

## Content Decisions Applied to the Site

The rewrite intentionally avoids these weak patterns:

- Inflated “everything company” positioning
- Invented futuristic services with low local-market credibility
- Huge undifferentiated service lists in the hero area
- AI-heavy claims without governance, security, or workflow context

The rewrite intentionally leans into these stronger patterns:

1. Narrower, more believable top-level categories
2. Chicago-specific managed IT language
3. Security and continuity as core, not add-ons
4. Microsoft 365 and cloud modernization as practical work
5. Roadmap-first discovery and phased rollout language
6. Premium design only where it supports trust and clarity

## Service Architecture Now Reflected in the Site

The primary service groups were reset to:

1. Managed IT and Support
2. Cybersecurity and Compliance
3. Cloud and Microsoft 365
4. Network and Infrastructure
5. Backup, Continuity and Recovery
6. Strategy, Automation and Workflow Systems

## Visual Direction

The homepage and header now use a Chicago skyline SVG to make the local positioning visible without turning the site into a tourism motif. The intent is local character with restrained execution.

## Ongoing Standard

Future edits should preserve this rule:

If a service does not show up repeatedly across leading Chicago MSPs or cannot be explained in one practical sentence tied to business outcomes, it should not be promoted as a primary offer.
