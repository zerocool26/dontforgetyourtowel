# Conversion Redesign Research - 2026-06-07

## Conversion Logic

The site should sell customer excellence before it sells technology. A serious MSP buyer wants to know:

- What services are actually included.
- How fast support is triaged.
- Who owns stuck issues.
- How security, backup, and Microsoft 365 are proven.
- What changes the monthly price.
- How the first 30 to 90 days become visible.

## Research Used

- Web performance is business trust: web.dev case studies connect Core Web Vitals improvements to better engagement, conversion, and bounce outcomes. Source: https://web.dev/case-studies/vitals-business-impact
- Form friction directly hurts conversion; Baymard recommends simpler, clearer form structures and fewer visible fields where possible. Source: https://baymard.com/learn/form-design
- Social proof is most useful when it is memorable and credible, especially testimonials with photos, recognizable logos, and press mentions. Source: https://cxl.com/research-study/social-proof/
- B2B buyers skim fast and need obvious routes, pricing context, contact paths, and proof. NN/g B2B usability research remains a strong reference point. Source: https://www.zenbacon.com/websites/B2B_usability.pdf

## Competitor Read

Chicago MSP competitors commonly emphasize:

- Flat-rate managed IT and predictable monthly pricing.
- Help desk, monitoring, cybersecurity, cloud, backup, and Microsoft 365.
- Response promises such as 1 to 4 hour non-critical response or faster emergency targets.
- Three-step onboarding or free assessment paths.
- Testimonials, partner badges, local coverage, and industry fit.

Examples reviewed:

- CTI Technology publishes broad managed IT inclusions and a $200 to $400 per user per month range. Source: https://www.ctinc.com/
- SafePoint IT emphasizes a 90-day guarantee, quick response, easy per-employee pricing, and customer testimonials. Source: https://www.safepointit.com/
- LeadingIT emphasizes one flat rate, unlimited support, local Chicago response, cybersecurity, backup, compliance, and co-managed IT. Source: https://goleadingit.com/
- Datastrive emphasizes a clear three-step switch process, fixed-price proposal, local coverage, and a short assessment form. Source: https://datastrive.com/

## Applied Decisions

- Recentered the homepage H1 around customer excellence and support expectations.
- Changed the main CTA from "View solutions" to "View services" for clearer intent.
- Replaced random experience/gallery framing with a customer excellence system.
- Added a competitor-aware offer comparison: market offer, buyer need, and site standard.
- Moved primary proof navigation toward the Trust Center instead of the old demo/showcase route.
- Reframed digital work as portals, intake, workflow, and client-facing service systems.
- Kept the hero visually energetic, but tied the energy to service motion and ownership.
