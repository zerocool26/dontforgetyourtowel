# Positioning application for CHICAGOS #1 MSP

## Position

CHICAGOS #1 MSP is an integrated Chicago technology firm for established
organizations that need senior guidance, production software, secure systems,
and accountable day-to-day operations without coordinating multiple vendors.

## Core promise

**Plan it. Build it. Secure it. Run it.**

This is the service model—not a decorative slogan:

| Stage  | Buyer question                            | Deliverable examples                                                                            |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Plan   | What should we change, and in what order? | Current-state review, roadmap, architecture, budget, vendor decision                            |
| Build  | What system or workflow will solve it?    | Product definition, UX, application, integration, data flow, test and launch plan               |
| Secure | How do we control risk from the start?    | Identity model, application security, cloud controls, recovery, evidence and response plan      |
| Run    | Who owns reliability after launch?        | Support, monitoring, updates, cloud operations, managed IT, documentation and improvement queue |

## Customer-facing service lanes

### Technology advisory

Technology roadmaps, architecture decisions, technical due diligence, vendor
selection, fractional technology leadership, and project recovery.

### Software & product engineering

Customer portals, internal systems, web applications, mobile-ready products,
APIs, integrations, modernization, quality engineering, and production handoff.

### Cloud & platform modernization

Microsoft 365, Azure and cloud foundations, identity, migrations, platform
engineering, observability, cost control, and legacy modernization.

### Cybersecurity & resilience

Security baselines, identity and access, endpoint and email security,
application security, backup, disaster recovery, incident readiness, and
governance.

### Managed IT & workplace

Service desk, endpoint management, onboarding and offboarding, networks,
Microsoft 365 administration, vendor ownership, lifecycle planning, and
co-managed support.

### Data, integration & automation

Data flows, reporting foundations, system integrations, workflow automation,
governed model-enabled workflows, and operational measurement.

## Homepage information architecture

1. Aggressive integrated promise and two actions.
2. Four-stage responsibility strip: Plan, Build, Secure, Run.
3. Buyer problem: fragmented responsibility across consultants, developers, and
   IT vendors.
4. Six service lanes with specific deliverables.
5. Delivery system from first review through ongoing operation.
6. Proof through working standards, not invented client metrics.
7. Direct contact handoff.

## Claims guardrails

- Keep `#1` as the registered brand expression, not a comparative market-share
  claim.
- Do not claim certifications, partner tiers, customer counts, uptime,
  satisfaction scores, awards, or response times unless verified evidence is
  supplied.
- Do not suggest every engagement includes 24/7 coverage; define coverage in the
  scope.
- Do not call every automation “AI.” Describe the workflow, controls, owner, and
  measurable result.
- Keep pricing directional and separate managed-service per-user planning from
  consulting and software project pricing.

## Practical differentiator

Global firms sell scale. The credible local advantage is tighter accountability:
the same operating model can carry a decision from roadmap to production and
then keep it supported, secured, documented, and improving.
