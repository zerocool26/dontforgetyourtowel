# Integrated technology services market research

Research date: August 13, 2026

## Purpose

This research supports the repositioning of CHICAGOS #1 MSP from a managed IT
specialist into an integrated technology consulting, managed services,
cybersecurity, cloud, data, and software engineering firm.

The goal is not to imitate global companies or borrow their claims. It is to
identify the offer architecture and buyer language used by large, commercially
successful providers, then apply the useful parts at a credible Chicago-market
scale.

## Selection method

The benchmark includes publicly traded companies with recent official financial
results and a meaningful overlap with at least two of these areas:

- technology consulting;
- custom software or digital engineering;
- cloud and platform modernization;
- cybersecurity;
- managed infrastructure or workplace services;
- ongoing application or business operations.

Revenue figures are included only as evidence that the operating model has
commercial scale. They are not an apples-to-apples ranking because reporting
periods, currencies, service mixes, and accounting categories differ.

Only official company, investor-relations, annual-report, earnings, and service
portfolio sources were used. All sources are linked directly and were accessed
on August 13, 2026.
