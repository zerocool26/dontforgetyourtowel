# Market leaders and adjacent operating models

## Accenture

Fiscal 2025 revenue was $69.7 billion, up 7% in both US dollars and local
currency. Accenture brought strategy, consulting, technology, operations, Song,
and Industry X into one integrated Reinvention Services unit. Its technology
scope explicitly spans cloud, systems integration, application management,
security, infrastructure, software engineering, and automation.

What matters here: one integrated commercial front door, deep capabilities
behind it, and a message centered on business change rather than a list of tools.

Sources:

- [Accenture 2025 Annual Report](https://www.accenture.com/content/dam/accenture/final/accenture-com/document-4/Annual-Report-2025.pdf)
- [Accenture fiscal 2025 financial performance](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/about/company/integrated-reporting-financial)
- [Accenture Reinvention Services announcement](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2025/accenture-changes-growth-model-to-reinvent-itself-for-the-age-of-ai)

## IBM

IBM reported $67.5 billion in 2025 revenue, up 6% at constant currency. Its
consulting offer is deliberately simplified into Strategy and Technology plus
Intelligent Operations. IBM describes the work as strategy, process design,
systems implementation, cloud architecture, application operations, and
security across the technology landscape.

What matters here: advisory and operations are not separate businesses. The
strategy team defines the change; the engineering and operations teams make it
work over time.

- [IBM 2025 Annual Report](https://www.ibm.com/investor/services/annual-report)

## Capgemini

Capgemini reported 2025 revenue of €22.465 billion, up 3.4% at constant exchange
rates. Its reporting structure provides a clear lifecycle: Strategy &
Transformation, Applications & Technology, and Operations & Engineering.
Applications & Technology represented 63% of revenue; Operations & Engineering
represented 29%.

What matters here: the lifecycle itself is the service architecture. Buyers can
enter at strategy, a build or modernization program, or ongoing operations
without being sent to unrelated brands.

Sources:

- [Capgemini full-year 2025 results](https://www.capgemini.com/us-en/news/press-releases/full-year-2025-results/)
- [Capgemini investor results package](https://investors.capgemini.com/en/publication/fy-2025-results/)

## Cognizant

Cognizant reported $21.1 billion in fiscal 2025 revenue. Its official services
portfolio combines consulting, application services, cloud, cybersecurity, data,
digital engineering, infrastructure services, and software engineering.

What matters here: software engineering is presented beside cloud, security,
and operations, not as an isolated creative studio. The digital engineering
offer also makes modernization, DevOps, product development, and platform
engineering concrete.

## CDW

CDW reported 2025 net sales of $22.424 billion, up 6.8%. Its value proposition
spans the full technology stack and lifecycle: advice, hardware, software,
professional services, managed services, cloud, security, and digital
transformation.

What matters here: lifecycle language reduces buyer anxiety. A customer can buy,
implement, operate, and improve technology through one accountable partner.

## Kyndryl

Kyndryl reported fiscal 2026 revenue of $15.1 billion. Kyndryl Consult revenue
was $3.5 billion, up 18% year over year. The company describes one model with
advisory, implementation, and managed service capabilities across modernization,
cloud, cybersecurity, and mission-critical operations.

What matters here: “advise, implement, manage” is a buyer-readable operating
model. It explains responsibility more clearly than an exhaustive capability
catalog.

## EPAM

EPAM reported 2025 revenue of $5.457 billion, up 15.4%. Its core offer is
Strategy, Engineering, Cloud, Cybersecurity, and Data & Analytics. EPAM's own
description emphasizes an evolution from software development into an integrated
consulting, design, and engineering firm.

What matters here: an engineering heritage can credibly expand into advisory and
operations when the company continues to show that its recommendations lead to
working systems.

## Globant

Globant reported 2025 revenue of $2.455 billion, up 1.6%, with a 15.2% adjusted

engineering, cloud operations, enterprise platforms, and custom software.

What matters here: the software work is described as a business capability—new
products, modernized platforms, and better operating systems—not merely access to
developers.

## Benchmark conclusion

The strongest shared model is not “we do everything.” It is a clear chain of
responsibility:

1. decide what should change;
2. design and build the right system;
3. secure and modernize the environment around it;
4. operate, support, and improve it after launch.

That model is broad without being vague because every stage has a distinct
buyer outcome and a visible handoff.
